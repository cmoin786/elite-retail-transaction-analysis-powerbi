# Elite Retail Transaction Analysis Dashboard


## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built on a retail transaction dataset from the UK.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue, profit, orders, customer segments, product categories, and payment methods to support business decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

* Analyze total revenue and profit performance.
* Monitor order volume and product quantity.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Understand customer loyalty segments.
* Evaluate payment method preferences.
* Track sales performance across multiple dimensions.

---

## Key Insights

* Electronics generated the highest revenue.
* Digital Wallet and Credit Card were the most preferred payment methods.
* Customer loyalty tiers were evenly distributed.
* Revenue and profit remained strong across major product categories.
